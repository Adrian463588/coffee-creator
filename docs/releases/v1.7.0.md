## What's New in v1.7.0

### 1. Offline Personal Palate Quiz & Editable Preference Profile (S2-063)
- 5 foundational taste dimensions evaluated offline:
  1. Roast Level Preference (Light $\to$ Dark)
  2. Acidity Tolerance (Bright Malic/Citric vs Soft/Muted)
  3. Sweetness Priority (High fruity vs Medium/Low)
  4. Bitterness Sensitivity (Hypersensitive low vs Tolerant high)
  5. Body & Mouthfeel (Delicate tea-like vs Syrupy heavy)
- Produces normalized continuous 5D Palate Vector with instant preference profile visualization and live manual sliders.

### 2. Bayesian Sensory Evidence Update Engine (S2-064)
- Personal taste preferences adapt dynamically from real cupping and journal sessions:
  $$\mathbf{p}_{\text{new}} = (1 - \alpha) \cdot \mathbf{p}_{\text{prior}} + \alpha \cdot \mathbf{x}_{\text{obs}}$$
- Filter gate: only sessions rated $\ge 7.50$ (or Q-score $\ge 80.0$) contribute sensory evidence.
- Mathematical invariants guarantee palate vector coordinates remain bounded in $[0.0, 1.0]$.

### 3. Explainable Local Bean & Recipe Recommendations (S2-065)
- Ranks inventory beans and starter recipes using multi-factor local scoring:
  $$\text{Score} = (\mathbf{p} \cdot \mathbf{b}) \times F_{\text{degas}}(\text{days}) \times G_{\text{avail}}$$
- Provides transparent human-readable explanation badges (e.g. *"Matches your high acidity preference (92% fit) • Peak degassing window (Day 12)"*).
- Completely offline without tracking or telemetry.

### 4. One-Variable-At-A-Time (OVAT) Brewing Feedback Loop (S2-066)
- Controlled recipe diagnostic engine following scientific single-variable adjustment rules:
  - Fast Drawdown + Sour $\to$ Grind 1 click finer
  - Normal Drawdown + Sour $\to$ Increase water temperature $+2.0^\circ\text{C}$ (clamped to boiling point)
  - Slow Drawdown + Bitter $\to$ Grind 1 click coarser
  - Normal Drawdown + Bitter $\to$ Decrease water temperature $-2.0^\circ\text{C}$
  - Weak / Watery $\to$ Increase coffee dose $+1.0\text{g}$
  - Harsh / Concentrated $\to$ Add $+20\text{g}$ water
- Generates an editable recipe draft with clear diagnostic reasoning; never mutates recipes in-place without confirmation.

### 5. Camera & OCR Coffee Bag Metadata Line Parser (S2-067)
- Offline text parser extracting roaster, origin country, region, variety, process, roast date, elevation (MASL), and net weight.
- Generates field-level extraction confidences.
- Strict review gate: whenever confidence $< 0.80$ or essential fields are missing, requires explicit user confirmation before committing to bean inventory.

### 6. Local AI Barista Chat & Schema-Validated Domain Tools (S2-068, S2-069, S2-070)
- Grounded conversational assistant running 100% locally with on-device knowledge base.
- Schema-validated tool execution:
  - `calculate_altitude_boiling_point`
  - `audit_ingredient_safety`
  - `propose_feedback_adjustment`
  - `recommend_inventory_bean`
  - `calculate_extraction_yield`
  - `convert_brix_to_tds`
  - `calculate_dilution_mass`
  - `calculate_aroma_percentage`
  - `save_recipe` (write operation strictly flagged with `requiresUserConfirmation = true`)
- Math performed strictly by pure JVM domain engine; zero model hallucinations in quantitative outputs.

### 7. RAG Document Chunk Citation & Strict Abstention Policy (S2-071)
- Answers cite specific local document chunks (`doc_name#chunk_id`).
- Strict abstention policy: when evidence similarity is below threshold or answering subjective/speculative queries without personal log evidence, explicitly abstains: *"No verified local document or observation supports this."*

### 8. Local Barista Persona Presets & Sanitization Controls (S2-072)
- Built-in barista persona presets:
  - **Specialty Q-Grader**: Rigorous, SCA-calibrated, precise sensory and scientific lexicon.
  - **Friendly Home Barista**: Welcoming, practical, approachable workflow tips.
  - **R&D Coffee Chemist**: Thermodynamics, extraction kinetics, molecular and mass-balance focused.
- Safe custom system prompt editor with length guard ($\le 300$ chars) and prompt injection sanitizer stripping system tokens, control tags, and override commands.

### 9. Offline User-Supplied Weather & Thermodynamic Boiling Point (S2-073)
- User-supplied elevation and ambient parameters without live GPS/weather APIs:
  $$T_{\text{boil}} = 100.0 - \left(\frac{h}{300}\right)^\circ\text{C}$$
- Automatically clamps brew water temperature recommendations to $T_{\text{target}} \le T_{\text{boil}} - 0.5^\circ\text{C}$ to prevent impossible or dangerous boiling water targets at high altitudes.

### 10. Food-Grade Aroma Ingredient & Allergen Safety Auditor (S2-074)
- Enforces strict food safety boundaries:
  - Rejects perfumery, cosmetic, or non-food grade chemicals (e.g. DPG, fragrance oils) with `CRITICAL_NON_FOOD_PROHIBITED` flags.
  - Verifies declared allergens (nuts, soy, dairy) and mandates explicit user acknowledgment before beverage consumption.
  - Validates mass fraction percentage against supplier limits.
