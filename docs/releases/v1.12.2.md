## Highlights & UI/UX Microinteraction Full-Max (`uiux-microinteraction-full-max`)

### 1. 2026 Tactile Industrial & Precision Coffee Lab Design System
- **Unified Tactile Primitives**:
  - `TactileButton`, `TactileOutlinedButton`, `TactileFilledTonalButton`, `TactileTextButton`, `TactileFilterChip`.
  - Physical scale micro-depression (`scale = 0.97f`) with spring physics (`Spring.DampingRatioMediumBouncy`, `Spring.StiffnessLow`).
  - Subtle physical haptic feedback (`HapticFeedbackType.TextHandleMove`) on every interactive touch target.
  - Strict minimum touch accessibility targets (48dp height/padding).
- **Concentric Layout Geometry**:
  - Strict adherence to concentric nested radii: \( R_{\text{inner}} = \max(0, R_{\text{outer}} - \text{Padding}) \) across all card containers and nested indicators.
  - 1px hairline border outline (`alpha = 0.40f`) on cards and surfaces, eliminating flat visual slop.

### 2. Complete Elimination of "AI-Slop" Across All Screens
- **Anti-Slop Single-Line Text Invariant**:
  - Enforced `maxLines = 1`, `softWrap = false`, and `TextOverflow.Ellipsis` across all buttons, chips, and telemetry badges.
  - Prevented awkward multi-line text wrapping and accidental circular bubble distortion.
- **Precision Data Readouts**:
  - Monospace tabular figures (`fontFeatureSettings = "tnum"`) for timers, flow rates (g/s), masses (g), extraction yield (%), and TDS (%).
- **Sleek Horizontal Scrollable Tabs & Chips**:
  - Replaced multi-row staggered FlowRows with sleek horizontal scrollable rows (`ScrollableTabRow` and horizontal `Row` with smooth scroll) across Library categories, Journal tabs, Journal method filters, and Sensory categories.
- **Precision Chronometer & Telemetry Console**:
  - High-contrast digital readout combining elapsed time tabular digits with secondary live mass and rolling flow rate (g/s).
  - Compact BLE scale status bar with quick Tare and Connect buttons.
