## 🚀 What's New in v1.14.7: Direct Hardware Haptics & Alarm-Grade Pour Reminders

### 1. 📳 Direct Hardware Haptic Feedback (`BrewVibrator`)
- **Direct Motor Actuation via `AudioAttributes.USAGE_ALARM`**:
  - Previously, timer vibration relied solely on standard `NotificationChannel.enableVibration(true)` which is automatically suppressed by Android when **Do Not Disturb (DND)**, **Bedtime mode**, or **Silent mode** is active, or when system touch haptics (`haptic_feedback_enabled = 0`) is turned off.
  - Implemented `BrewVibrator` using direct `Vibrator` / `VibratorManager` HAL APIs with `AudioAttributes.USAGE_ALARM`. Android treats brew step transitions as critical kitchen/barista timer alarms, actuating the physical vibration motor at full amplitude (`1.000000`) regardless of system touch haptic or DND suppression.
- **Rhythmic Tactical Vibration Signatures**:
  - `STEP_TRANSITION`: 2 crisp pulses (`[0ms, 180ms, 100ms, 200ms]`, ~480ms).
  - `TARGET_TIME`: 3 decisive pulses (`[0ms, 250ms, 100ms, 250ms, 100ms, 350ms]`, ~950ms).
  - `COMPLETED`: 1 solid confirmation pulse (400ms).
  - `CLICK`: 40ms micro-pulse with `USAGE_ALARM` for instant tactile toggle confirmation.

### 2. 🔊 Alarm-Grade Audio Engine (`BrewAudioPlayer`)
- **Independent Audio Channel with `USAGE_ALARM`**:
  - Plays system notification and alarm ringtones directly with `AudioAttributes.USAGE_ALARM`, ensuring audio alerts cut through ambient café/grinder noise and sound accurately at each step.
  - Exception-safe lifecycle handling with instant resource release on session pause or service destroy.

### 3. 🧪 "Test alert" CTA & Immediate Tactile Feedback
- **Live Alert Verification Button**:
  - Added a dedicated **"Test alert"** button (`Modifier.testTag("timer:test_alert")`) inside the Pour reminders console. Baristas can immediately verify their phone's vibration motor and speaker before beginning a brew.
- **Instant Toggle Haptics**:
  - Toggling "Vibration" ON immediately fires a confirmation pulse (`STEP_TRANSITION`).
  - Toggling "Sound" ON plays an immediate audio confirmation click.

### 4. ⚡ Dual-Redundant Single-Authority Dispatch & Decoupled Notifications
- **Zero Race Condition between Foreground UI and Service**:
  - Both `CoffeeViewModel` (active screen) and `BrewTimerService` (background/sleep) route cue playback through `graph.brewTimer.claimCue(cue)`. Whichever entity executes first claims the vibration and audio, preventing duplicate rings.
- **Decoupled Notification Posting (`lastNotifiedCueId`)**:
  - System status-bar notifications are decoupled from the cue claim flag, ensuring `BrewTimerService` always posts the high-importance step notification even if the foreground ViewModel processed the tactile vibration first.
