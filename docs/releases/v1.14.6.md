## 🚀 What's New in v1.14.6: Replay, Precision Typography, Step Cues & Semi-Auto Espresso

### 1. 🔁 1-Click Replay from Journal & Catalog directly to Timer
- **Instant Replay Flow**:
  - Tapping **Brew again** or clicking on any Journal card or World Recipe card (`journal:repeat:${entry.id}`) instantly prepares the brew session, preloads the exact recipe draft, and navigates directly to the Timer screen.
- **Active Brew Session Guard**:
  - Protects running or paused brew sessions from accidental overwriting. Replay requests while a timer is active prompt with an explicit warning or refusal rather than silently clobbering live barista data.
- **Zero Stale Measurement Leakage**:
  - Replayed sessions cleanly decouple previous measurement outcomes (previous TDS %, beverage mass, extraction yield, rating, tasting notes). The chronometer starts fresh at 00:00 with target parameters ready for new measurement.

### 2. 📐 Anti-AI-Slop Responsive Fact Cards (No Squishing, Isolated Ratio Row)
- **Eliminated Character-Wrapped Fact Blobs**:
  - Replaced crowded single-row fact layouts with a responsive $2 \times 2$ grid (Coffee Mass, Water Mass, Water Temperature, Brew Time).
  - Isolated the **Ratio & Bypass** specification onto its own dedicated row (`1:15.0 (+ bypass to 155g)`), preventing text collisions and vertical character distortion.
- **Adaptive FontScale Protection**:
  - Automatically collapses to single-column stacking when system accessibility font scaling exceeds $1.5\times$ or on narrow viewport widths ($< 360\text{dp}$).
- **Monospace Tabular Figures (`tnum`)**:
  - Numerical readouts formatted with `fontFeatureSettings = "tnum"` for rock-solid visual column alignment without numeral jitter.

### 3. ⏱️ Progressive Pour Step Disclosure & "Continue to Timer" CTA
- **Compact Step Summary Cards**:
  - Pour steps are presented as clean, non-bloated summary cards (`+60.0 g · 45 s · target 60.0 g · 92°C`).
  - Tapping an individual step expands progressive inline controls for that specific step rather than unfolding 4 inputs per step simultaneously.
- **Direct Barista Action**:
  - Prominent **"Continue to timer"** button at the top of the step list allows fast transition from review to active brewing with zero excess clicks.

### 4. 🔔 Pouring Step Cues & Background Foreground Service
- **User-Selected Cue Modalities**:
  - Haptic vibration, notification sound, or silent visual cues configurable in preferences.
  - Distinct vibration rhythm for step transitions vs final session target time.
- **Foreground Service (`specialUse` FGS)**:
  - Keeps chronometer and step cues active when screen sleeps or barista switches apps.
- **Deduplicated Cues**:
  - Monotonic tick engine ensures no cue fires twice within the same second.

### 5. ☕ Semi-Automatic Espresso Support (Xiaomi, Nuova Simonelli Appia, Ferratti Ferro)
- **Pure Manual Stopwatch Mode**:
  - Timer functions as a true barista manual chronometer. When target shot time (e.g., 28s) is reached, it signals a target cue but continues counting until the barista manually taps Finish.
- **Zero Mandatory BLE Hardware**:
  - Machine profile serves as display metadata only; no forced Bluetooth scale pairing or imaginary automated machine control.
