## Sprint 04 Highlights: Sensory Training & Testing Lab (Epic C: `S4-027` to `S4-040`)

### 1. Blind Triangle Discrimination Drills (`S4-027`, `S4-028`, `S4-029`)
- **Rigorous Triad Structure (`S4-027`)**:
  - Exactly 3 coded samples per trial: 2 reference samples + 1 odd sample.
  - Uniform pseudo-randomization across all 6 spatial permutations: `AAB`, `ABA`, `BAA`, `BBA`, `BAB`, `ABB`.
- **Concealed Answer Key (`S4-028`)**:
  - Arbitrary 3-digit blind sample numbers (e.g. `#402`, `#718`, `#891`) decoupled from true sample identities.
  - Zero answer reveal or odd-cup disclosure until the user submits their choice.
- **Trial Metadata & Conditions (`S4-029`)**:
  - Full tracking of trial number, randomization seed, operator identity, washout duration, confidence rating (1–5★), serving temperature, and lighting conditions.

### 2. Statistical Psychophysics Engine (`S4-030`, `S4-038`)
- **Exact Binomial Hypothesis Testing (`S4-030`)**:
  - Implements cumulative upper-tail binomial test against null probability $p_0 = 1/3$:
    $$P(X \ge k) = \sum_{i=k}^n \binom{n}{i} \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^i \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n-i}$$
  - Critical value tables for $\alpha = 0.05, 0.01, 0.001$.
  - Sensory fatigue alerts and statistical caveats for small sample sizes ($n < 6$) and trial dependence.
- **2-AFC Paired Comparisons & Ranking (`S4-038`)**:
  - Dedicated directional paired comparison testing against null probability $p_0 = 1/2$.
  - Strictly decoupled from 3-sample triangle discrimination tests.

### 3. Five Basic Tastes & Threshold Calibration (`S4-031`, `S4-032`)
- **Standardized Reference Solutions (`S4-031`)**:
  - Complete formulation protocols for all 5 basic tastes: Sweet (Sucrose), Sour (Citric Acid), Salty (NaCl), Bitter (Caffeine), and Umami (MSG).
  - 4 standardized concentration levels: Detection, Low, Medium, and High (g/L).
  - Solvent purity specification (< 30 ppm TDS deionized/distilled water) and strict FCC/USP food-grade safety alerts.
- **ASTM E679 Ascending Concentration Series (`S4-032`)**:
  - 3-Alternative Forced Choice (3-AFC) ascending dilution series.
  - Automated calculation of Best-Estimate Threshold (BET) via geometric mean:
    $$\text{BET} = \sqrt{\Delta C_i \times \Delta C_{i+1}}$$
  - Distinct detection threshold (detecting presence of a stimulus) vs. recognition threshold (identifying specific taste quality).
  - Mandatory non-clinical disclaimer: Educational calibration only; does not diagnose clinical gustatory impairments.

### 4. Olfactory Memory & Spaced Repetition (`S4-033`, `S4-034`)
- **Physical Reference Vials (`S4-033`)**:
  - Standardized physical reference sets (Fruit, Floral, Vegetable, Roast, Spice, Nutty, Sweet, Defects) with concealed answer keys.
- **Sensory SM-2 Algorithm & Calibration (`S4-034`)**:
  - Automated spaced-repetition scheduling:
    $$EF' = EF + (0.1 - (5 - q) \times (0.08 + (5 - q) \times 0.02))$$
  - Real-time confusion matrix calculation tracking descriptor substitutions and misidentifications.
  - Brier score confidence calibration assessing overconfidence vs. underconfidence in sensory calls.

### 5. Organic Acids & Multi-Modal Chemistry (`S4-035`)
- **7 Organic Acid Comparison Drills**:
  - Systematic comparison: Citric, Malic, Tartaric, Acetic, Phosphoric, Quinic, and Lactic acids.
  - Exact stock concentration recipes (g/L), pKa1 values, and flavor descriptors.
  - Mandatory food-grade verification and supervisor co-signing workflow.
  - Prominent educational disclaimer: Sourness is a basic taste; perceived coffee acidity is a multi-modal combination of organic acid chemistry, aroma, and mouthfeel.

### 6. Roast Defects & Tactile Discrimination (`S4-036`, `S4-037`)
- **Roast Defect Identification (`S4-036`)**:
  - Controlled blind evaluation against calibrated reference coffee: Underdeveloped (grassy/cereal), Baked (flat/bready), Scorched (ashy/bitter), and Quaker (peanutty/dry).
  - Explicit documentation of causal uncertainty (avoiding false single-variable roast attribution).
- **Tactile / Mouthfeel Discrimination (`S4-037`)**:
  - Systematic training for body intensity, texture (tea-like, silky, velvety, creamy, syrupy), and astringency.
  - Strict serving temperature (40°C–65°C) and water mineral TDS controls.

### 7. Readiness Radar & Human Observation Mandate (`S4-039`, `S4-040`)
- **6-Pillar Sensory Readiness Dashboard (`S4-039`)**:
  - Multi-dimensional skill calibration across Triangle, 5 Tastes, Olfactory, Organic Acids, Roast Defects, and Tactile.
  - Longitudinal performance tracking and weakness diagnostics.
  - Prominent disclaimer: Does not confer official SCA or CQI certification credentials.
- **Human Observation Anti-Cheating Mandate (`S4-040`)**:
  - Strict domain invariant: `PracticalExerciseVerification.isAutoPassed` is permanently `false`.
  - Practical exercises cannot be auto-passed by virtual reading or quizzes alone; require physical tasting trials and verifiable interaction.
