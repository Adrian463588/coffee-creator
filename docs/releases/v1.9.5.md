## Highlights & Sensory Cupping Deepening (Epic S2-E05)

### 1. Interactive Multi-Ring Canvas Sensory Wheel (`SensoryWheelVisualizer.kt`)
- **Hardware-Accelerated Sunburst Wheel**: Rendered via Compose `Canvas` with high-contrast color tokens and clean geometry.
- **Inner & Outer Ring Architecture**:
  - Inner Ring ($r \in [r_{in}, r_{mid}]$): 9 Primary Sensory Categories (Floral, Fruity, Sour/Fermented, Green/Vegetative, Roasted, Spices, Nutty/Cocoa, Sweet, Defects & Taints).
  - Outer Ring ($r \in [r_{mid}, r_{out}]$): 26 Subcategories and 90+ individual flavor descriptors.
- **Touch-Arc Polar Coordinate Hit Detection**:
  - Calculates polar angle $\theta = \text{atan2}(dy, dx)$ and radius $r = \sqrt{dx^2 + dy^2}$ to identify tapped slice.
  - Interactive slice zoom/isolation: Tapping a primary category zooms into that category's subcategories and descriptors; tapping center resets to full wheel.
- **Accessibility & TalkBack Integration**:
  - Embedded TalkBack semantics description on each slice, providing full non-visual accessibility parity.

### 2. Comprehensive 90+ Flavor Wheel Taxonomy (`FlavorWheelTaxonomy.kt`)
- **Authoritative Lexicon Integration**:
  - Mapped directly to SCA / World Coffee Research (WCR) Sensory Lexicon and Indonesian *Roda Rasa Kopi* standards.
  - Expanded from 42 to **90+ distinct descriptors** across 9 primary categories and 26 subcategories.
- **Dedicated Professional QC Defect / Taint Category (`cat_defects_taints`)**:
  - Added essential commercial cupping defects: Potato Defect (PTD), Phenolic/Carbolic, Rubber, Petroleum, Medicinal/Bandaid, Baggy/Karung Goni, Cardboard, Past Crop, Earthy, Musty/Apek, Humus, Stinker Bean, Onion, Rioy.
- **Fast Domain Lookup APIs**:
  - `findDescriptorByName(name)`
  - `findCategoryById(id)`
  - `findSubcategoryById(id)`
  - `getDescriptorsForCategory(catId)`
  - `getDescriptorsForSubcategory(subcatId)`

### 3. Multi-Select Flavor Wheel Picker Dialog (`FlavorWheelPickerDialog`)
- **Integrated Modal Selection**: Searchable, categorizable multi-descriptor picker modal with active chip removal and batch commit.
- **Cupping Sample Integration**: Cuppers can tap `"Pick from Wheel"` in the `Score Sample` dialog to attach standard sensory descriptors directly to cupping sheets.
- **Brew Journal Integration**: Added `"Wheel Notes"` button on `JournalTab.BREWS` to directly update and tag brew journal entries with standard wheel descriptors.

### 4. Dynamic Template Cupping Scoring
- **Dynamic Attribute Sliders**: In the `Score Sample` dialog, attributes dynamically adapt based on `session.template.type`:
  - **Specialty SCA Equivalent** (10 attributes): Fragrance/Aroma, Flavor, Aftertaste, Acidity, Body, Balance, Uniformity, Clean Cup, Sweetness, Overall (6–10 scale + defect deduction).
  - **Quick Barista Dial-In** (6 attributes): Aroma, Acidity, Sweetness, Body, Balance, Overall (1–10 scale).
  - **Production QC** (4 attributes): Roast Defect, Flavor/Cleanliness, Acidity/Structure, Sweetness/Finish (1–10 scale).
  - **Espresso Sensory** (6 attributes): Crema/Aroma, Crema Persistence, Tactile/Body, Acidity/Brightness, Sweetness, Finish (1–10 scale).
- **Deterministic Math**: Pure Kotlin mathematical engine evaluates final score based on template type without floating-point artifacts.
