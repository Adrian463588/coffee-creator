## Sprint 04 Highlights: Green Coffee Physical Assessment (Epic D: `S4-041` to `S4-051`)

### 1. Comprehensive Green Lot Catalog & Chain of Custody (`S4-041`)
- **Rigorous Lot Metadata**:
  - Full tracking of species (`Coffea Arabica`, `Coffea Canephora`, `Coffea Liberica`, `Coffea Eugenioides`), processing methods (Washed, Natural, Honey, Wet Hulled, Experimental).
  - Provenance details: Country, Region, Farm/Estate, Producer, Harvest Crop Year, Lot ID, and Standard Sample Mass (strictly 350.0g).
  - Chain of Custody: Exporter, Importer, Warehouse Terminal, Container ID, and ambient storage conditions.

### 2. SCA 2004 Washed Arabica Defect Taxonomy & Weighted Scoring (`S4-042`, `S4-043`)
- **Primary vs. Secondary Defect Classification**:
  - **Category 1 (Primary Full Defects, 1:1 Ratio)**: Full Black, Full Sour, Dried Cherry / Pod, Fungus Damaged, Foreign Matter, Severe Insect Damage.
  - **Category 2 (Secondary Defects, Weighted Ratios)**: Partial Black (3:1), Partial Sour (3:1), Parchment (5:1), Floater (5:1), Immature / Unripe (5:1), Withered (5:1), Shell (5:1), Broken / Chipped / Cut (5:1), Hull / Husk (5:1), Slight Insect Damage (10:1).
- **Physical Hazard & Disqualification Rules**:
  - Foreign matter and large stones/sticks flagged as physical hazard risks.
  - A single Category 1 defect immediately disqualifies the sample from Specialty Grade 1 status.
  - Category 2 defects are weighted by exact verified equivalent ratios ($\le 5.0$ full equivalents for Specialty Grade 1).
  - Photo attachment support for physical defect evidence logging.

### 3. Sieve Size Distribution & Mass Balance Check (`S4-044`)
- **ISO 4150:2010 Standard Sieve Stack**:
  - Standardized screen fractions: Screen 18, Screen 17, Screen 16, Screen 15, and Pan (< Screen 12).
  - Mechanical/manual shaking duration protocol (3 minutes for 350g sample).
- **Mathematical Mass Balance Recovery Validation**:
  - Strictly validates recovered mass against initial sample mass ($|\sum m_i - M_0| \le 1.5\text{g}$).
  - Automatically calculates cumulative Screen 16+ retention percentage ($\ge 85\%$ required for Specialty Grade 1 size uniformity).

### 4. Moisture, Water Activity & Physical Lab Telemetry (`S4-045`, `S4-046`)
- **Instrument-Grounded Moisture Assessment (`S4-045`)**:
  - Meter model (e.g. Sinar AP 6060, Dickey-John mini GAC), serial number, calibration date, method (Dielectric Capacitance vs. ISO 6673 Loss on Drying).
  - Sample temperature (°C), instrument uncertainty ($\pm 0.2\%$).
  - Zero fabricated readings: nullable moisture strictly defaults to `NOT_MEASURED`.
- **Water Activity & Bulk Density (`S4-046`)**:
  - Water activity ($a_w$) measurement with critical microbiological safety threshold ($a_w \le 0.700$ to suppress xerophilic mold and ochratoxin A risk).
  - Bulk density measurement (ISO 6669 free-flow cylinder in g/L, $\ge 700\text{g/L}$ for SHB classification).
  - Organoleptic odor inspection with mandatory disqualification for musty, moldy, phenolic, or chemical off-odors.

### 5. Deterministic Grading Engine & Out-of-Scope Guards (`S4-047`, `S4-051`)
- **SCA 2004 Washed Arabica Grade Rule Engine**:
  - Evaluates grades: Specialty Grade 1, Premium Grade 2, Exchange Grade 3, Below Standard Grade 4, and Off Grade 5.
  - Moisture range enforcement: Washed Arabica must fall strictly between $9.0\%$ and $13.0\%$.
- **Strict Out-of-Scope Refusal (`S4-047`, `S4-051`)**:
  - The SCA 2004 protocol applies exclusively to Washed Coffea Arabica.
  - Evaluating Robusta/Canephora, Liberica, or Natural/Dry processed lots returns `UNDETERMINED_OUT_OF_SCOPE` rather than applying incorrect Arabica rules.
  - Explicit missing parameter states: `NOT_MEASURED`, `UNKNOWN`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, `PENDING_REVIEW` with ZERO data fabrication.

### 6. Epistemological Decoupling & Dual Specialty Verification (`S4-048`)
- **Physical Quality $\ne$ Sensory Outcome**:
  - Physical green grading evaluates agricultural/post-harvest defect cleanliness.
  - Sensory CVA cupping evaluates beverage hedonic and descriptive flavor.
  - Neither can overwrite or substitute for the other:
    - High sensory score (e.g. 88.0 pts) cannot bypass a Full Black or Foreign Matter disqualification.
    - Grade 1 physical cleanliness cannot grant specialty status to a woody, past-crop coffee scoring $< 80.0$ pts.
  - Both standards must independently pass for the lot to receive verified `DUAL_SPECIALTY` status.

### 7. Buyer Contract Specification Compliance (`S4-049`)
- Parameter-level verification against custom buyer acceptance limits:
  - Max Category 1 defects, Max Category 2 equivalents.
  - Min/Max moisture percentage.
  - Max water activity ($a_w \le 0.700$).
  - Min Screen 16+ retention percentage ($\ge 85\%$).
  - Clean odor requirement.
- Clear parameter-level PASS / FAIL checklist with overall contract acceptance evaluation.

### 8. Tamper-Evident QC Export & Audit Trail (`S4-050`)
- **Standardized Export Formats**:
  - App-native Green QC JSON export sealed with cryptographic SHA-256 hash.
  - ISO-compliant Green QC CSV export for laboratory information systems (LIMS) and logistics.
  - Complete audit trail of assessor identity, equipment calibration dates, and standard version attribution.
