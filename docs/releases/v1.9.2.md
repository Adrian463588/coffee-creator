## Highlights & Anti-Slop Tactile Enhancements

### 1. Journal Screen Anti-Slop Navigation
- **Eliminated Multi-Row Staggered Wraps**: Replaced 3-line staggered wrapping `FlowRow` chips with a single-line horizontal scrollable row (`Row + horizontalScroll(rememberScrollState())`) for the 6 primary Journal categories (`Brews & Logs`, `Espresso Dial-In`, `Compare (Select)`, `Analytics & Streak`, `Cafe Cups`, `Export & Tools`).
- **Tactile Filter Chips**: Integrated `TactileFilterChip` with spring micro-depression physics (`scale = 0.96f`, `DampingRatioMediumBouncy`) and M3 icons (`Icons.Default.Book`, `Icons.Default.Tune`, `Icons.Default.CompareArrows`, `Icons.Default.BarChart`, `Icons.Default.LocalCafe`, `Icons.Default.FileDownload`).
- **Single-Line Method Filter Bar**: Replaced wrapping method filters with a sleek horizontal scroll row (`All`, `V60`, `Espresso`, `AeroPress`, `Chemex`, `High Rated`), saving over 80dp of vertical viewport height.

### 2. Sensory Wheel & Cupping Screen Anti-Slop Navigation
- **Single-Line Sensory Mode Tabs**: Replaced multi-row chips with a smooth horizontally scrollable row for `Flavor Wheel`, `Accessible List`, `Cupping Sessions`, `Consensus & Radar`, `Physical QC & Rest`, and `Calibration Lab`.
- **Single-Line Flavor Taxonomy Categories**: Replaced 3-row staggered wrapping category chips with a fluid horizontal scroll row covering all 10 taxonomy categories (`All`, `Fruity`, `Floral`, `Sweet`, `Nutty/Cocoa`, `Spices`, `Roasted`, `Vegetal`, `Sour/Fermented`, `Other`).
