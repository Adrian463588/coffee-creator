### 7. Blind Panel Workflow & Audited Unblinding (`S4-058`)

  - Host emergency reveal requires explicit reason entry and logs an immutable audit event: `UNBLINDED_PREMATURELY_AUDITED`.

### 8. Inter-Rater Agreement & Panel Calibration Analytics (`S4-059`)
- **Comprehensive Statistical Calibration Suite**:
  - **Pearson Product-Moment Correlation ($r$)**: Evaluates continuous/interval score alignment between assessors. Includes small-sample ($N \ge 2$) and zero-variance guards ($s_x^2 > 0 \land s_y^2 > 0$).
  - **Spearman Rank Correlation ($\rho$)**: Computes ordinal correlation with fractional mid-ranks for tied values.
  - **Mean Consensus Deviation (MCD)**: Assesses distance from panel consensus mean:
    $$\text{MCD}_k = \frac{1}{S} \sum_{s=1}^S |x_{k,s} - \mu_s|$$
    Target benchmark: $\text{MCD} \le 0.35$ indicates high panel calibration.
  - **Fleiss' Generalized Kappa ($\kappa_{\text{Fleiss}}$)**: Computes multi-rater categorical agreement across Clean vs Taint vs Fault classifications, mapped to Landis & Koch (1977) benchmarks (`POOR`, `SLIGHT`, `FAIR`, `MODERATE`, `SUBSTANTIAL`, `ALMOST_PERFECT`).
