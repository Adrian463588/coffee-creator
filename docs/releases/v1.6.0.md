## What's New in v1.6.0

### 1. Hierarchical Searchable Flavor Wheel & Accessible List (S2-051)
- 9 Primary categories: Fruity, Floral, Sweet, Nutty/Cocoa, Spices, Roasted, Cereal, Fermented, Green/Vegetative.
- 80+ curated descriptors with nested subcategories, exact color hex tokens, sensory notes, and synonyms.
- High-contrast, TalkBack screen reader compliant Accessible List alternative with semantic roles and touch targets >= 48dp. No feature depends solely on visual color hue.

### 2. Descriptor Reference Calibration Exercises & Food Safety Boundaries (S2-052)
- Built-in palate training reference recipes:
  - Citric Acid ($0.50\text{ g/L}$ in $1000\text{ mL}$) for lemon/lime acidity.
  - DL-Malic Acid ($0.40\text{ g/L}$ in $1000\text{ mL}$) for green apple acidity.
  - Pure Sucrose ($10.00\text{ g/L}$) for sweetness perception and viscosity.
  - Quinine HCl ($0.005\text{ g/L}$ / $5\text{ mg/L}$) with strict 50 mL daily consumption safety limit and pregnancy/cinchona allergy warnings.
  - Anhydrous Caffeine ($0.20\text{ g/L}$) for coffee bitterness calibration.
- Dynamic batch volume scaling calculator computing exact mass in grams down to $0.0001\text{ g}$.

### 3. Standard Sensory Sliders & Q-Grade Score Aggregation (S2-053, S2-055)
- Standard sensory attributes: Fragrance/Aroma, Flavor, Aftertaste, Acidity, Body, Balance, Uniformity, Clean Cup, Sweetness, Overall.
- Cupping Sheet Templates:
  - Specialty 100-Point Protocol (6.00 to 10.00 in 0.25 steps).
  - Quick Barista Dial-In (1.0 to 10.0 continuous).
  - Roastery Production QC (Profile fidelity, cleanliness, development).
  - Espresso Extraction Sensory (Crema persistence, viscosity, lingering finish).
- Specialty score summation with standard defect deductions: $\text{Defects} = (\text{TaintCups} \times 2) + (\text{FaultCups} \times 4)$.

### 4. Epistemological Origin & Provenance Badges (S2-054)
- Strict epistemological boundary enforced across UI and data layer:
  1. `HUMAN_OBSERVATION`: Empirical cupper score, 100% confidence, solid radar stroke, no disclaimer.
  2. `AI_HEURISTIC_HYPOTHESIS`: Uncalibrated directional rule (dashed stroke, mandatory disclaimer: *"Heuristic assumption; validate by observation. No measured laboratory accuracy claimed."*). Never written to official cupping ledger.
  3. `CALIBRATED_PREDICTION`: Statistical model with explicit confidence interval (e.g. `8.25 [7.95 - 8.55] (95% CI)`).

### 5. Cupping Session Manager & Blind Masking (S2-056, S2-057)
- Multi-sample cupping sessions with evaluator panel assignment (Lead Cupper, Q-Grader, Guest Evaluator, Barista).
- Masking Modes: `OPEN_LABEL`, `SINGLE_BLIND_EVALUATOR` (evaluators see code, lead sees truth), and `DOUBLE_BLIND_PANEL` (all parties see code until controlled reveal).
- Controlled reveal toggle showing bean name, origin, process, and roaster only when authorized.

### 6. Session Lifecycle & Immutable Audit Trail (S2-058)
- State machine: `DRAFT` $\to$ `OPEN` $\to$ `CLOSED` $\to$ `REOPENED`.
- Locking prevents score tampering after formal close.
- Reopening requires explicit reason, logged immutably into session audit log with timestamp and editor ID.

### 7. Multi-Evaluator Consensus & Dispersion Analytics (S2-059)
- Consensus statistics: Arithmetic Mean ($\bar{x}$), Median ($\tilde{x}$), Sample Standard Deviation ($s$).
- **Inter-Rater Agreement Index (IRAI)**: $\text{IRAI} = \max(0.0, 1.0 - \frac{s}{\sigma_{\text{threshold}}})$, quantifying panel calibration.
- Statistical outlier flagging ($|x - \bar{x}| > 2s$).
- Consensus Descriptor Cloud: selection counts and percentage frequency.
- Pairwise Jaccard Overlap Matrix: $J(A, B) = \frac{|D_A \cap D_B|}{|D_A \cup D_B|}$.

### 8. Offline QC Report & Cryptographic Receipt (S2-060)
- One-tap export to Markdown and CSV summary.
- Computes SHA-256 cryptographic receipt fingerprint over full session metadata, evaluator scores, and physical QC data.
- 100% offline generation with zero network access.

### 9. Physical Green & Roasted QC Integration (S2-061)
- Standard 350g green sample evaluation: Moisture %, Water activity ($a_w$), Bulk density (g/L), and defect counts.
- Instant grading: Specialty Grade 1 (Strict) vs Premium Grade 2 vs Commercial based on full black/sour primary and secondary defect penalties.
- Roasted Agtron Gourmet whole bean and ground measurements with computed roast delta: $\Delta\text{Agtron} = \text{Ground} - \text{WholeBean}$.

### 10. Roast-Rest Degassing Lifecycle & Peak Flavor Window (S2-062)
- Rest curve prediction modeling $\text{CO}_2$ desorption kinetics:
  - Light roast peak flavor window: Day 10 to 24 post-roast.
  - Medium roast peak flavor window: Day 6 to 18 post-roast.
  - Dark roast peak flavor window: Day 3 to 10 post-roast.
- Multi-observation timeline mapping degassing phases: `Fresh / Degassing (CO2 interference)`, `Peak Maturity`, `Stable Post-Peak`, and `Stale / Oxidized`.
