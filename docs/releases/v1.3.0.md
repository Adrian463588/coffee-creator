# Coffee Creator v1.3.0 — Release Notes

**Epic S2-E02: Recipe Composer and Deterministic Brewing Science (Stories S2-013 to S2-026).**
This distribution APK is debug-signed, targets Android 8/API 26+, and supports arm64-v8a/x86_64 architectures. Zero cloud dependencies.

## Key Changes in v1.3.0

### 1. Recipe Composer & Lifecycle (S2-013, S2-014, S2-015)
- **Lifecycle Operations**: Full create, edit, duplicate, archive, unarchive, and versioning for recipes.
- **Entity Linking**: Explicit linking to local beans and grinding/brewing equipment.
- **Brew Method Presets**: 12 standard methods supported (Espresso, Pour Over V60, AeroPress, Chemex, French press, Cold brew, Immersion, Batch brew, Moka pot, Siphon, Cupping, Custom).
- **Custom Methods & Stages**: Support for custom apparatus names and stage sequences (e.g. Hario Switch hybrid open/closed stages).

### 2. Ordered Brew Steps & Scaling Engine (S2-016, S2-017)
- **Ordered Brew Steps Editor**: Dedicated "Steps" tab in the Brew section to view and edit steps with action, water amount, duration, cumulative mass, temperature, and valve status.
- **Exact Hamilton-Hare Apportionment Scaling**: Quick scaling buttons (0.5x, 1.5x, 2.0x) or custom scaling that recomputes all individual step targets using the largest-remainder method, guaranteeing that the step sum matches the target water mass exactly with zero rounding drift, and maintains strictly monotonic cumulative mass.

### 3. Brewing Science & Refractometer Lab (S2-018, S2-019, S2-020, S2-021, S2-022, S2-023)
- **Optical Brix→TDS Refractometer Lab**: Interactive converter card in the Measurements tab supporting documented calibration profiles:
  - Standard Coffee (slope 0.85)
  - Atago PAL-COFFEE (slope 0.84)
  - VST LAB Coffee III (slope 0.857)
  - Generic Sucrose Equivalent (slope 0.85)
- **Direct Mass-Balance Extraction Yield**: Pure Kotlin science calculation $EY\% = \frac{m_{\text{beverage}} \times \text{TDS}\%}{m_{\text{dose}}}$ with strict input validation.
- **Concentrate Dilution & Solution Dosing**: Mass-balance dilution calculator and target solution dosing solver.
- **Food-Grade Aroma Dosing**: Exact separation of stock addition % ($w/w$) from active compound ppm with supplier limit enforcement.
- **Physical Safety Bounds**: Parameter validation enforcing safe temperature (0–100°C), dose (0.5–1000g), water (5–20000g), and extraction pressure ($\le 15$ bar).
- **Temperature Conversions**: High-precision conversions between Celsius and Fahrenheit.

### 4. Expert Mode & Dial-In Parameters (S2-025)
- Dedicated "Expert" tab in the Brew section:
  - First drop timing (seconds)
  - Grinder model and clicks / dial setting (e.g. Comandante, EK43S)
  - Water chemistry: General Hardness (GH ppm) and Carbonate Alkalinity (KH ppm)
  - Basket & Puck Prep notes (WDT, distribution tool, paper filter placement)

### 5. Curated Starter Catalog & Offline Sharing (S2-024, S2-026)
- **Curated Starter Recipes**: 10 source-attributed competition and world-standard recipes with ordered pour routines.
- **Zero-Cloud Recipe Sharing**: One-tap export via Android Sharesheet to:
  - Formatted Barista Specification Card (`text/plain`)
  - Structured Recipe Specification (`application/json`)
