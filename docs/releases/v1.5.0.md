## What's New in v1.5.0

### 1. Detailed Brew Journal (S2-039) & Multi-Dimensional Search (S2-040)
- Full parameter preservation: dose (g), water (g), ratio (1:X), grind size, water temp (°C), brew duration (s), drawdown (s), measured TDS (%), extraction yield (%), sensory rating (1-10), taste tags, notes, and local photo content URIs.
- Instant search across bean name, method, notes, and multi-dimensional filter chips (V60, Espresso, AeroPress, Chemex, High-Rated >= 8.5).

### 2. Side-by-Side Session Comparison with Deterministic Deltas (S2-041)
- Select any two brew logs into Slot A and Slot B to compute exact numerical deltas:
  - $\Delta \text{Dose}$ (g), $\Delta \text{Water}$ (g), $\Delta \text{Ratio}$, $\Delta \text{Duration}$ (s), $\Delta \text{TDS}$ (%), $\Delta \text{EY}$ (%), $\Delta \text{Rating}$ (points), and $\Delta \text{Water Temperature}$ (°C).
  - Calculated using `BigDecimal` half-up rounding.

### 3. Espresso Target vs Actual Dial-In & OVAT Coach (S2-042, S2-043)
- Shot kinematic progression tracking: target vs actual dose, yield, extraction duration, and mean flow rate ($g/s$).
- **One-Variable-at-a-Time (OVAT) Guidance**: Recommends strictly ONE physical parameter modification per iteration based on extraction kinetics and sensory flags:
  - *Channeling detected* ($<20\text{s}$ or dual sour/astringent flags) $\to$ Puck Prep correction (WDT, uniform tamp).
  - *Under-extracted / Sour* ($EY < 18.0\%$) $\to$ Grind 1 step finer.
  - *Over-extracted / Bitter* ($EY > 22.0\%$) $\to$ Grind 1 step coarser.
  - *Weak / Low TDS* $\to$ Tighten ratio (decrease water yield or increase dose).
  - *Strong / High TDS* $\to$ Widen ratio or apply measured bypass water.

### 4. Interactive Charts & Brewing Control Quadrants (S2-044)
- Aggregated time-series flow curves and 2D scatter coordinates $(TDS\%, EY\%)$ mapped against SCA Gold Cup quadrants.

### 5. Local Streak, Calendar & Monthly Analytics (S2-045)
- Active day walking algorithm computing current brewing streak with a 1-day grace period.
- Historical longest streak tracking.
- Monthly consumption aggregation: total brews logged, total coffee dose consumed (g), preferred brewing method, and average extraction yield.

### 6. Quick Repeat Last Brew & Template Persistence (S2-046)
- One-tap "Repeat Last Brew" pre-populates previous dose, water, grind, and temperature directly onto the active brew chronometer.
- Save any high-scoring journal entry as a reusable template in the Recipe Library.

### 7. CSV Export & Backup Conflict Detection (S2-047)
- Standardized CSV export via Android Sharesheet (`RecipeExportFormatter.shareJournalCsv`).
- 3-Way SHA-256 backup restoration preview identifying `NEW`, `IDENTICAL`, and `MODIFIED_REMOTE_NEWER` records before committing writes.

### 8. Beanconqueror JSON Migration (S2-048)
- Automated schema parser importing Beanconqueror JSON backups directly into Coffee Creator beans and brew journal logs.
- Automatic 1–5 to 1–10 rating normalization.

### 9. Cafe Cup Journal (S2-049)
- Local artisan coffee journal for logging cafe visits, roasters, drink names, prices, and ratings.
- Zero remote map SDKs, zero GPS queries, 100% offline.

### 10. Caffeine Intake Estimator with Uncertainty Labels (S2-050)
- Species-calibrated mass balance: Arabica (~1.20% dry caffeine) vs Robusta (~2.20% dry caffeine).
- Extraction efficiency model (85% nominal, 80-90% bounds).
- Prominent non-clinical scientific disclaimer: *"Heuristic estimation; actual caffeine varies by bean variety, agricultural terroir, roast degree, and extraction dynamics. No clinical or laboratory diagnostic accuracy is claimed."*
