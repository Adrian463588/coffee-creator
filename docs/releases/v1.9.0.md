## What's New in v1.9.0

### 1. Bean Agronomic Metadata & CRUD (S2-001)
- Rich coffee bean records capturing roaster, origin country, region, farm, producer, varietal, processing method, altitude (masl), harvest date, and roast date.
- Full offline search and multi-criteria filtering across the bean inventory.

### 2. Bag Inventory Lifecycle & Dose Deduction (S2-002)
- Multi-bag management per bean with distinct lifecycle states: `PURCHASED`, `ACTIVE`, `FINISHED`, and `FROZEN`.
- Accurate mass-balance deduction on brewing with automatic status transition to `FINISHED` when remaining mass reaches $0.00\,\text{g}$.
- Safety guard: brewing is strictly rejected if a bag is currently frozen until explicitly thawed.

### 3. Roast-Rest Calculator & Explainable Freshness (S2-003)
- Heuristic roast-rest engine determining effective aging days based on roast level and processing method (e.g. natural vs washed degassing requirements).
- Explainable freshness states:
  - `TOO_FRESH_DEGASSING`: High CO2 release, uneven extraction risk.
  - `PEAK_FLAVOR`: Optimal aromatics and balanced solubility window.
  - `STABLE_MATURE`: Steady extraction phase.
  - `STALE_OXIDIZED`: Loss of volatile aromatics.
- Freeze pause mechanics: active freeze durations are deducted from chronological days so frozen beans pause their aging clock.

### 4. Grinder Records & Burr Calibration (S2-004)
- Cataloging manual and electric grinders with calibration unit abstractions: `CLICKS`, `MICRONS`, `STEPLESS_DEGREES`, and `NUMERICAL_MARKS`.
- Detailed burr profiles: conical vs flat burrs, burr diameter (mm), burr material (steel, ceramic, titanium), and zero-point offset tracking.

### 5. Multi-Category Equipment Catalog (S2-005)
- Standardized gear catalog spanning: `DRIPPER`, `FILTER_PAPER`, `KETTLE`, `SCALE`, `ESPRESSO_MACHINE`, `PORTAFILTER_BASKET`, `PUCK_PREP`, `IMMERSION_BREWER`, `GRINDER`, and `OTHER`.
- Equipment metadata includes nominal capacity, flow characteristics, filter geometry, and manufacturer notes.

### 6. Water Chemistry Engine & Mineral Profiles (S2-006)
- Comprehensive water profile modeling: $\text{Ca}^{2+}$, $\text{Mg}^{2+}$, $\text{Na}^+$, $\text{HCO}_3^-$, and $\text{pH}$.
- Molar mass conversions calculating Total Hardness ($GH$) and Total Alkalinity ($KH$) as $\text{ppm CaCO}_3$:
  $$GH = \text{Ca}^{2+} \times \frac{100.0869}{40.078} + \text{Mg}^{2+} \times \frac{100.0869}{24.305}$$
  $$KH = \text{HCO}_3^- \times \frac{100.0869}{2 \times 61.0168}$$
  $$\text{TDS} = \text{Ca}^{2+} + \text{Mg}^{2+} + \text{Na}^+ + \text{HCO}_3^-$$
- Curated water presets included: SCA Standard, Rao/Perger Recipe, Hendon Balance, and Pure Distilled / RO.

### 7. Food-Grade Ingredient Catalog & Safety Verification (S2-007)
- Catalog of additives and aroma solutions with exact density ($g/\text{mL}$), declared allergens, and supplier maximum dosage limits (% w/w).
- Strict food-safety gate: non-food-grade and unverified ingredients are blocked from being added to beverage recipes.

### 8. Bag Photo Attachment & Bundled Offline OCR (S2-008)
- Local heuristic OCR parsing lines into structured bean drafts: roaster, coffee name, origin country, process method, altitude, roast date, and bag mass.
- Confidence scoring per field with an interactive manual review gate before persistence.

### 9. Bulk Import/Export with Conflict Resolution (S2-009)
- Bulk JSON catalog archive and CSV export for beans and equipment.
- Dry-run import inspection previewing new, identical, and conflicting records.
- Deterministic conflict strategies: `OVERWRITE`, `KEEP_BOTH_RENAME`, and `SKIP_DUPLICATE`.

### 10. Global Offline Search, Tags, and Favorites (S2-010)
- Instant tokenized search matching name, origin, roaster, process, tags, and notes across beans, grinders, and equipment.
- One-tap favorite toggling with prioritized listing.

### 11. Green Coffee Physical QC & Defect Grading (S2-011)
- Physical green coffee analysis: moisture content (%), bulk density ($g/\text{L}$), water activity ($a_w$), and screen size.
- Full defect tracking according to specialty coffee standards: Category 1 (Full Black, Sour, Fungus) and Category 2 (Partial Black, Broken, Insect).
- Automatic Specialty Grade 1 evaluation (0 Category 1 defects, $\le 5$ Category 2 defect points, moisture 9–13%).

### 12. Roast Batches, RoR Curves, and Blends (S2-012)
- Roast batch logging with charge temperature, drop temperature, yellowing/first crack milestones, and Development Time Ratio (DTR %):
  $$\text{DTR} = \frac{\text{Drop Time} - \text{First Crack Time}}{\text{Drop Time}} \times 100\%$$
- Instantaneous Rate-of-Rise (RoR, $^{\circ}\text{C}/\text{min}$) calculation from time-temperature curve data points.
- Multi-component blend formulation with strict 100% mass sum invariant.
