### 2. Standard 16-Attribute CVA Professional Cupping Protocol
- **Complete Coffee Value Assessment (CVA) Attributes**:
  - `fragrance`: Orthonasal dry grounds evaluation (0–15).
  - `aroma`: Orthonasal wet crust break evaluation (0–15).
  - `flavor`: Retronasal & taste combination at slurp (0–15).
  - `aftertaste`: Length, clarity, and quality of finish (0–15).
  - `acidity_intensity`: Quantitative intensity (0–15).
  - `acidity_quality`: Qualitative categorization (`CITRIC`, `MALIC`, `TARTARIC`, `PHOSPHORIC`, `ACETIC`, `LACTIC`, `COMPLEX`).
  - `sweetness_intensity`: Sweetness level (0–15).
  - `bitterness_intensity`: Bitterness level (0–15).
  - `saltiness_intensity`: Mineral / savory detection (0–15).
  - `body_intensity`: Perceived mouthfeel weight (0–15).
  - `body_texture`: Qualitative tactile description (`WATERY`, `TEA_LIKE`, `SILKY`, `VELVETY`, `CREAMY`, `SYRUPY`, `OILY`, `CHALKY`).
  - `astringency`: Tactile drying / puckering sensation (0–15).
  - `finish_duration_s`: Quantitative aftertaste duration in seconds.
  - `temperature_c`: Exact evaluation temperature at time of tasting.
  - `defects`: Qualitative taint and fault flags with weighted deduction calculus (`taint × 2 + fault × 4`).
  - `overall_impression`: Affective and holistic assessment (1–9).

### 3. Multi-Phase Temperature Cupping Timeline
- **Sensory Progression Tracking**: Captures distinct cupping observations across the cooling curve:
  1. `DRY_FRAGRANCE` (Ground coffee before water)
  2. `WET_AROMA` (Steeping crust before break)
  3. `CRUST_BREAK` (Aroma burst at 4:00 minute break)
  4. `INITIAL_HOT_CUPPING` (~70°C, initial slurp)
  5. `WARM_CUPPING` (~60°C, sweetness & acidity development)
  6. `COOL_CUPPING` (~40°C, body & defect clarity)
  7. `FINAL_AFTERTASTE` (Linger & astringency evaluation)
