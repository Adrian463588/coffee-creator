### 3. Sensory Training Lab
- **Balanced Triangle Testing Engine**:
  - Strictly presents 3 coded samples (2 identical + 1 different) utilizing all 6 balanced presentation orders: `AAB`, `ABA`, `BAA`, `BBA`, `BAB`, and `ABB`.
  - Conceals the correct key until the evaluator submits their choice.
- **Exact Binomial Probability Calculus**:
  - Computes cumulative binomial probability:
    $$P(X \ge k) = \sum_{i=k}^n \binom{n}{i} \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^i \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n-i}$$
  - Statistical significance flagged at $\alpha = 0.05$ (e.g., $k=8$ correct out of $n=15$ trials yields $p = 0.0433 \implies \text{Significant}$).
- **Basic Taste Calibration Guidelines**:
  - Food-grade lab recipes for Sucrose (sweet), Citric Acid (sour), Sodium Chloride (salty), Caffeine (bitter), and Monosodium Glutamate (umami).
- **Roast Defect Recognition**:
  - Systematic training for Baked, Scorched, Underdeveloped, and Quaker roast defects.
