## Sprint 04 Highlights: Digital CVA Cupping & 221-Descriptor Wheel Integration (Epic B: `S4-011` to `S4-026`)

### 1. Multi-Sample Setup & Equipment / Preparation Metadata (`S4-011`)
- **Multi-Sample Cupping Sessions**:
  - Full session management with custom or randomized 3-digit blind sample codes (e.g. `#402`, `#718`, `#891`).
  - Evaluator identity and pseudonym controls with timestamp logging.
  - Controlled equipment metadata: Grinder model, Burr type, Kettle model, Water TDS (ppm), Grind size microns, and Bowl capacity (mL).
  - Controlled sample preparation: Water temperature (°C), Dose grams, Water mass, Ratio, and Roasting Agtron score.

### 2. Protocol Selector & Integrity Badges (`S4-012`, `S4-026`)
- **Protocol Separation**:
  - Prominent visual mode badges: `CVA 2024 STANDARD`, `LEGACY 2004`, `CUSTOM RESEARCH`.
  - Dedicated warning banners for historical 2004 100-point cupping and custom research modes.
  - Strict epistemological barrier: **No silent or implicit conversion between 2004 100-point total scores and CVA descriptive profiles**.

### 3. 4-Perspective Decoupled Cupping Forms (`S4-013` – `S4-016`)
- **Physical Form (`S4-013`)**:
  - Official field mapping: Roast color (Agtron commercial/gourmet, RoastQuik), bean size screen distribution, moisture %, and quakers per 100g.
- **Descriptive Form (`S4-014`)**:
  - 0.0 to 5.0 intensity sliders across 9 attributes: Fragrance (dry), Aroma (wet crust), Flavor, Aftertaste, Acidity, Sweetness, Bitterness, Mouthfeel Body, and Finish Duration.
  - Qualitative acidity characterization (Citric, Malic, Phosphoric, Tartaric, Acetic, Lactic).
  - Qualitative mouthfeel texture (Watery, Tea-like, Silky, Velvety, Creamy, Syrupy, Oily, Chalky).
  - Strictly non-hedonic; no invented total score.
- **Affective Form (`S4-015`)**:
  - Hedonic quality scale (6.00 to 10.00 or 1-9 points) strictly isolated from descriptive intensity.
- **Extrinsic Form (`S4-016`, `S4-017`)**:
  - Traceability metadata: Origin, Region, Farm, Altitude (masl), Variety, Process, Certifications, Lot price/kg.
  - Strict blinding gate: Extrinsic reveal requires explicit user authorization, logging an audited `UNBLINDED_PREMATURELY_AUDITED` event.

### 4. Full 221-Descriptor Sensory Wheel Integration (`S4-014`)
- **Comprehensive Taxonomy Modal**:
  - Instant access to the complete 221-descriptor flavor database (84 official SCA/WCR reference descriptors + 137 expanded Indonesian and regional descriptors).
  - Searchable by English and Indonesian terms (e.g. `Lemon`, `Melati`, `Teh Hitam`).
  - Filterable by 9 primary families: Floral, Fruity, Sour/Fermented, Green/Vegetative, Roasted, Spices, Nutty/Cocoa, Sweet, Other/Defects.
  - Multi-select chips with instant add/remove feedback, embedded into the Descriptive cupping assessment.

### 5. 5-Bowl Multi-Cup Evaluation Grid (`S4-021`)
- **Multi-Cup Defect & Uniformity Tracking**:
  - Interactive 5-bowl grid per sample.
  - One-tap state cycle: `Clean (✓)` $\to$ `Taint (-2 pts)` $\to$ `Fault (-4 pts)` $\to$ `Clean (✓)`.
  - Automatic calculation of Clean Cup score ($10.0 - \text{defect pts}$) and Uniformity score ($10.0 - 2.0 \times \text{defective count}$).

### 6. Guided Cupping Stepper & Timing Engine (`S4-018`)
- **6 Cupping Milestones**:
  1. Dry Grounds Fragrance
  2. Hot Water Pour (93.5°C)
  3. Crust Break & Aroma Burst (Target 4:00 min)
  4. Skim & Hot Cupping (~70°C)
  5. Warm Cupping (~60°C)
  6. Cool Cupping & Final Assessment (~40°C)
- Non-blocking countdown timer with haptic and audio cues.

### 7. Cryptographic Lock, Amendments & Export (`S4-022`, `S4-023`, `S4-024`)
- **Immutable SHA-256 Seal**:
  - Locks completed cupping sessions with cryptographic hash verification.
  - Audited revision amendments ($R+1$) preserving original records with mandatory reason documentation.
- **App-Native Data Export**:
  - One-tap export to standardized CSV and JSON formats with official legal disclaimers.

### 8. Side-by-Side A/B Coffee Comparison (`S4-025`)
- Compare any two samples in a session side-by-side.
- Computes intensity deltas ($\Delta$) and highlights shared vs. unique wheel descriptors.

### 9. 1-Click "Log to Brew Journal as Graded Coffee"
- Direct bridge from Cupping Lab to Brew Journal: Creates an immutable `BrewJournalEntry` with cupping scores, tasting notes, ratings, and wheel descriptors.
