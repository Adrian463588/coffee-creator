## Tactile Microinteractions & Anti-AI-Slop Refinement (`uiux-microinteraction-sprint03-refinement`)

This release refines the UI/UX across all Sprint 03 surfaces (44-Model Grinder Catalog, 26-Dripper Catalog, Brew Composer equipment telemetry, and Espresso Dial-In) adhering strictly to Anti-AI-Slop and 2026 Precision Coffee Lab standards:

### 1. Semantic `MicroBadgePill` Architecture
- **Elimination of Raw Text Brackets**:
  - Replaced crude bracketed plaintext like `[HAND] · [ROTATION] · [MANUFACTURER_VERIFIED]` and `[CONE_60] · [Flow: UNKNOWN]` with structured, semantic `MicroBadgePill`s.
  - **Color Semantics**:
    - Grinder Type: Hand Grinder (Bronze / Crema), Electric Grinder (Purple / Indigo).
    - Verification Status: Manufacturer Verified (Emerald Green outline and text), User Supplied (Amber warning).
    - Special Flags: "Filter Only" warning badge for grinders unsuited for espresso (Fellow Ode Gen 2, ZP6 Special).
    - Valve State: Valve Equipped indicator (Violet / Purple).
  - **Single-Line Invariant**: Enforces `maxLines = 1`, `softWrap = false`, and `overflow = TextOverflow.Ellipsis` on all badge pills, preventing vertical bubble expansion.

### 2. 3-Tile Monospace Grind Range Readout
- Replaced plain text range descriptions with a structured 3-tile grid (`Coarse (Tubruk)`, `Medium (V60)`, `Fine (Espresso)`).
- Tabular monospace numbers (`fontFeatureSettings = "tnum"`) prevent numeric jitter.
- Green checkmark (`✓`) prominently highlights verified ranges.

### 3. Animated Segmented Valve Switch (`InteractiveValveSwitch`)
- Replaced plain text button (`Valve: OPEN (Tap to CLOSE)`) with an interactive, segmented pill switch:
  - `OPEN (Percolation)` vs `CLOSED (Immersion)`.
  - Spring-animated thumb with scale micro-depression (`0.97f`) on press.
  - Haptic touch feedback (`TextHandleMove`) on physical tap.

### 4. Step-by-Step Zero Calibration Procedure
- Replaced raw newline-delimited bullet points with a structured, numbered procedure card (1, 2, 3...) inside a 1px hairline container (`outlineVariant.copy(alpha = 0.40f)`).
- Tactile "Hide Zero Guide" dismissal with spring physics.

### 5. Layout Squeezing & Anti-Slop Fixes
- Fixed letter-by-letter vertical wrapping on dripper drain speed and grind baseline.
- Elevated Espresso Dial-In progression in Journal with 3-tile metrics (`Dose`, `Yield`, `Time`) and structured `MicroBadgePill`s for grinders, settings, flow rate, and TDS.
- Elevated Recipe Composer dripper flow advice with structured callout containers.
