## Sprint 03 Highlights: Manual Brewing & Grinder Intelligence (`sprint-03-manual-brew-grinder-intelligence`)

### 1. Grinder Intelligence Engine (`GR-01` to `GR-12`)
- **44-Model Grinder Catalog (`GR-01`)**:
  - Full curated catalog of 44 leading hand and electric coffee grinders (Comandante C40 MK3/MK4, Timemore C2/C3/Chestnut, 1Zpresso JX/JX-Pro/K-Max/ZP6, Fellow Ode Gen 1/2, Baratza Encore/ESP/Sette 270, Eureka Mignon Specialita, Mahlkönig EK43, Mazzer Super Jolly/Philos, Niche Zero/Duo, Option-O Lagom P64/mini, DF64/DF83, Kinu M47, Kingrinder K2/K4/K6, Wilfa Svart, Weber EG-1, etc.).
  - Searchable by brand, model, mechanism (`HAND` vs `ELECTRIC`), burr geometry, and status.
- **Grinder Model Variants & Invariants (`GR-02`)**:
  - Distinct tracking of burr type (conical, flat), burr diameter (mm), burr material (stainless steel, titanium coated, DLC), axle bearings, and adjustment unit (`CLICKS`, `ROTATIONS_AND_CLICKS`, `NUMERICAL_DIAL`, `MICRONS`, `STEPLESS_DEGREES`).
  - Strict invariant: No silent cross-model conversion (e.g., C40 clicks are never converted to 1Zpresso clicks).
- **Interactive Zero Calibration Wizard (`GR-03`)**:
  - Safe, step-by-step model-specific zero finding instructions (burr lock prevention, audible rub check).
  - Saves calibration zero point offset with timestamp and burr condition notes.
- **Typed Grind Settings (`GR-04`)**:
  - Type-safe representation with bounds checking according to each grinder's adjustment mechanism.
- **Manufacturer Evidence Overlay (`GR-05`)**:
  - Displays verified manufacturer recommendation ranges alongside community-verified user settings; conflicting values are both preserved with citation notes.
- **User Grinder Profile Management (`GR-06`)**:
  - Save owned grinders, current burr condition (new, seasoned, worn), zero offset, and calibration log.
- **Grind Range Finder (`GR-07`)**:
  - Filter by grinder model, brewer, and brew method to provide starting point recommendations strictly grounded in catalog evidence.
- **Reversible Dial-In Assistant (`GR-08`)**:
  - Pure Kotlin extraction physics engine providing reversible step adjustments (+1/-1 click) based on user taste feedback (sour -> finer, bitter -> coarser, channeling -> inspect puck/coarser) without deterministic taste claims.
- **Grind History Integration (`GR-09`)**:
  - Automatically records grinder revision, setting, and offset in brew journal logs for searchable comparison.
- **Grinder Compatibility Guard (`GR-10`)**:
  - Protects specialized brew grinders (e.g. Fellow Ode Gen 2, 1Zpresso ZP6 Special) from incompatible espresso requests, surfacing clear warnings.
- **Grind Measurement Metadata (`GR-11`)**:
  - Optional Kruve sieve / laser diffraction micron particle size fields only displayed when measured by the user.
- **Calibration Profile Transfer (`GR-12`)**:
  - Type-safe JSON export and import with schema versioning (`schemaVersion = 1`).

### 2. Manual Brewing & Equipment Intelligence (`BR-01` to `BR-18`)
- **26-Dripper Curated Catalog (`BR-01`)**:
  - Curated database of 26 pour-over, immersion, and hybrid drippers (Hario V60 01/02/03, Kalita Wave 155/185, Chemex 6-Cup, Origami Dripper S/M, Hario Switch 02/03, Clever Dripper, Fellow Stagg [X]/[XF], Orea V3/V4, April Brewer, Kinto Slow Coffee, Melitta, Bee House, Torch Mountain/Donut, Gabi Master A, Aeropress, Tricolate, Cafec Flower, Timemore Crystal Eye, Loveramics Brewers, Pulsar NextLevel, Gina Goat Story, Graycano, Sibarist Fast, Saint Anthony C70).
  - All unknown physical parameters remain explicitly `UNKNOWN`.
- **Brewer Geometry & Flow Mode (`BR-02`)**:
  - Conical, flat bottom, trapezoid, immersion, hybrid.
  - Interactive valve toggle (`OPEN` for percolation, `CLOSED` for full immersion).
- **Filter Paper Compatibility Engine (`BR-03`)**:
  - Detailed compatibility matrix for cone, wave, trapezoid, flat circular, and Sibarist fast papers.
- **Manual Brew Composer (`BR-04`, `BR-05`, `BR-06`)**:
  - Interactive gear selection, ratio scaling (1:12 to 1:18), water temperature guidance, bloom mass calculation (2x to 3x dose), and staged pour planning (pulse vs continuous).
- **Offline Chronometer & Telemetry (`BR-07`, `BR-08`, `BR-09`)**:
  - Tabular countdown/elapsed timer, pause/resume, BLE scale flow rate (g/s) with fallback manual weigh-ins, and diagnostic observation recording (drawdown time, clogging, bypass, channeling).
- **Mineral Water & Recipes (`BR-10`, `BR-11`, `BR-12`)**:
  - User water hardness / buffer tracking, 9 pour-over methods, 80 World Champion recipes.
- **Sensory Vocabulary & Experiment Compare (`BR-13`, `BR-14`)**:
  - 221-descriptor flavor wheel linking, A/B side-by-side brew comparisons with delta calculus.
- **Local AI Tools (`BR-15`)**:
  - Typed Kotlin tools: `lookup_grinder_range`, `recommend_dial_in_step`, `lookup_dripper_geometry`.
- **Accessibility & Backward Compatibility (`BR-16`, `BR-18`)**:
  - 48dp touch targets, TalkBack descriptors, zero data loss migration for Sprint 01/02 records.
