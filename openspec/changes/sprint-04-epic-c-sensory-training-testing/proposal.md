# OpenSpec Proposal: Sprint 04 EPIC C — Sensory Training & Testing (S4-027 to S4-040)

## Context & Objectives
Implement an offline-first, mathematically rigorous Sensory Training & Testing engine in Coffee Creator.
Grounds coffee sensory training in psychophysical standards (ISO 4120, ASTM E679, ISO 8586, SCA Sensory Education):
1. **Triangulation Discrimination Engine**: 6-permutation balanced presentation, concealed keys, and exact upper-tail cumulative Binomial probability calculation against $p_0 = 1/3$.
2. **Gustatory & Olfactory Calibration**: Five basic tastes with FCC/USP reference solutions, ascending concentration thresholding (3-AFC), olfactory family identification with physical vials/food references, spaced repetition memory (SM-2), and confusion matrix analytics.
3. **Comparative Practice Disciplines**: Organic acid discrimination under food-grade safety guidelines, roast defect recognition against calibrated control coffees, tactile mouthfeel evaluation under temperature/serving controls, and 2-AFC paired comparisons / ranking exercises.
4. **Pedagogical & Ethical Invariants**: Personalized readiness radar across 6 pillars, strict human observation verification gate (never auto-pass based on reading or virtual quizzes), and unambiguous disclaimers stating that the application does not confer official SCA, CQI Q-Grader, or R-Grader credentials.

## Scientific Framework & Mathematical Models

### 1. Cumulative Binomial Probability Model (S4-030)
In a triangle test with $n$ independent trials, an assessor chooses the odd sample out of 3 cups.
Under the null hypothesis $H_0$ (pure guessing), the probability of correct selection is $p_0 = \frac{1}{3}$.
For $k$ correct responses out of $n$ trials, the exact one-tailed cumulative $p$-value is:
$$P(X \ge k) = \sum_{i=k}^n \binom{n}{i} \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^i \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n-i}$$
where:
$$\binom{n}{i} = \frac{n!}{i!(n-i)!}$$
- For Paired Comparison (2-AFC, S4-038), $p_0 = \frac{1}{2}$:
$$P(X \ge k) = \sum_{i=k}^n \binom{n}{i} \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^n$$
- Significance thresholds: $\alpha = 0.05$ (statistically significant), $\alpha = 0.01$ (highly significant).
- Methodological guards: Warn if $n < 6$ (insufficient statistical power). Explicitly document that repeated testing on identical coffee causes sensory adaptation, fatigue, and memory bias.

### 2. Ascending Concentration Series & Threshold Model (S4-032)
Based on ASTM E679 3-AFC ascending concentration methodology:
Concentration step $m \in \{0, 1, \dots, M-1\}$ follows a geometric progression:
$$C_m = C_0 \cdot r^m$$
where $C_0$ is baseline detection concentration and $r$ is dilution factor (default $r = 2.0$ or $1.5$).
- Detection threshold: lowest concentration where sample is reliably distinguished from water.
- Recognition threshold: lowest concentration where the specific basic taste is correctly identified.
- Individual Best-Estimate Threshold (BET):
$$\text{BET} = \sqrt{C_{\text{last\_incorrect}} \cdot C_{\text{first\_correct}}}$$
- Non-clinical guard: Threshold calculations represent sensory calibration only and MUST NOT infer clinical sensory impairment (hypogeusia, ageusia).

### 3. Olfactory Spaced Repetition (Sensory SM-2) & Confusion Matrix (S4-034)
Spaced repetition for aroma memory based on modified SuperMemo SM-2:
$$I_1 = 1 \text{ day}, \quad I_2 = 3 \text{ days}, \quad I_n = \text{round}(I_{n-1} \cdot EF)$$
Ease Factor update on response quality $q \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$:
$$EF' = \max\left(1.3, EF + (0.1 - (5 - q) \cdot (0.08 + (5 - q) \cdot 0.02))\right)$$
Confusion Matrix $M_{K \times K}$ where entry $M_{ij}$ records times true aroma $i$ was identified as aroma $j$:
$$\text{Precision}_i = \frac{M_{ii}}{\sum_{j=1}^K M_{ji}}, \quad \text{Recall}_i = \frac{M_{ii}}{\sum_{j=1}^K M_{ij}}, \quad F1_i = 2 \cdot \frac{\text{Precision}_i \cdot \text{Recall}_i}{\text{Precision}_i + \text{Recall}_i}$$
Confidence Calibration (Brier Score):
$$BS = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{t=1}^N \left(\frac{c_t}{5.0} - o_t\right)^2$$
where $c_t \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ is confidence rating and $o_t \in \{0, 1\}$ is correctness. Lower Brier score ($BS \to 0$) indicates superior calibration.

## Pure Domain Architecture (`com.coffeecreator.app.core.sensory.training`)
Encapsulates all models in pure JVM Kotlin without Android SDK, Room, or Compose dependencies.

## Invariants & Non-Goals
- **100% Offline-First**: Zero cloud dependencies, zero external analytics, full airplane mode operation.
- **Zero AI Hallucination / Anti-AI Slop**: No simulated flavor predictions or automated cupping scores generated by LLM.
- **Strict Non-Certification**: Unambiguous legal disclaimer on every report and dashboard screen.
- **Human Observation Mandate**: Practical sensory skills require real physical preparation and tasting.
- **Safety First**: Food-grade ingredients only; analytical balance weighing; poison/toxic lab chemical prohibitions.
